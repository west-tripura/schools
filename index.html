Platform Features

Built for Field-Level Efficiency

Every feature is designed to reduce paperwork, improve compliance tracking, and give district administrators real-time visibility.

🔐

Secure Role-Based Login

Email + password authentication with OTP-based password reset. Admins and inspectors see different views based on their role.

📄

Instant PDF Generation

Submit a form and get a formatted inspection PDF emailed instantly. Download directly from the dashboard.

💾

Draft Auto-Save

Never lose progress — inspection forms are saved as drafts mid-way through. Resume anytime from any device.

📈

Admin Dashboard

Aggregated KPIs for the entire district — water access %, PM-POSHAN compliance, student-teacher ratio, and comparative inspector analytics.

🏆

Inspector Comparative Study

Side-by-side performance comparison of up to 5 school inspectors with metrics, progress bars, and downloadable records.

📱

Works Offline (PWA)

Install on your phone or tablet and access the app without an internet connection for field inspections.
